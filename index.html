Neither sticker was the bill.

Korean skincare ships from Korea or Hong Kong, and since the US ended its duty-free exemption, every parcel pays a tariff. Every store still draws the line differently: free-shipping thresholds, flat rates, how the duty lands on your bill. K-Save does this arithmetic for every product, every store, every day. Here it is for the same bottle, twice:

For our retail partners.

What K-Save is

A comparison engine: a mobile app (iOS & Android) that computes the delivered cost of Korean skincare across five stores and routes ready-to-buy shoppers to the store that wins.

How traffic flows

Every product page ends in a click-out to the retailer's own site, affiliate-tracked where a program exists. The retailer owns checkout, payment and fulfilment; K-Save never resells or intermediates.

Where prices come from

Network product datafeeds, refreshed daily, normalized to USD, with the shipping-and-tariff rulebook applied per store. Stale feeds are flagged, never silently shown.

Who's on the other end

US-based K-beauty buyers: price-sensitive enthusiasts and quiz-led newcomers arriving at the exact moment they've decided what to buy.
